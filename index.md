# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-04  
**App Name:** Cosmic Ocean (Android)

## Summary
Cosmic Ocean stores your data locally on your device. We do not collect, share, or sell personal data. There is no account system and no cloud synchronization.

## Data We Collect
We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, or identifiers from your device. All task data and settings are stored locally on the device.

## Data We Share
We do not share any data with third parties.

## Data Storage and Security
All app data is stored on-device. You can delete the app at any time to remove locally stored data. The app does not transmit your data to any server.

## Permissions
If the app requests permissions (such as notifications), they are used solely to enable local features on your device and are not used to collect or share data.

## Children’s Privacy
Cosmic Ocean does not knowingly collect data from children. The app is designed to operate without data collection.

## Changes to This Policy
If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date and provide the updated policy in the app or on the website where this policy is hosted.
